Covergroups

axil_cgAXI4-Lite access

Samples operation, all 14 register addresses plus unmapped access, WSTRB class and pattern, individual byte lanes, and response.

  • Crosses operation × address.
  • Crosses staging-register address × WSTRB pattern.
10 points2 crosses
descriptor_cgDescriptor properties

Samples length classes, source/destination alignment, 4-KiB crossing, overlap, and TAG class from observed staging writes.

  • Length bins cover zero through 0xfffff.
  • Boundary matrix covers neither, source, destination, and both-side crossing.
7 points1 cross
irq_cgInterrupt behavior

Samples IRQ level and transitions, enable state, and completion-valid state.

The two crosses cover enable × IRQ and completion-valid × IRQ.

4 points2 crosses
manager_status_cgStatus register

Samples every implemented STATUS bit: active, request empty/full/ready, completion valid/full, IRQ, rejection sticky bits, mismatch, and empty pop.

11 points
queue_level_cgFIFO occupancy

Samples empty, one, mid, and full levels for both four-entry FIFOs. Out-of-range levels are illegal bins.

The presence cross records all empty/non-empty request and completion combinations.

4 points1 cross
completion_cgCompletion records

Samples TAG and length classes plus implemented read/write error encodings and mismatch state.

  • Read errors: none, SLVERR, DECERR.
  • Write errors: none, SLVERR, DECERR.
5 points
outstanding_cgOutstanding bursts

Samples zero, one, and multiple incomplete read and write bursts after each AXI monitor-clock update.

The check phase records maximum read and write depths of at least two.
